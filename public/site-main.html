FAQ

Questions,
answered gently

Is MindShift+ a replacement for therapy?

No, and we're clear about that. MindShift+ is a wellness companion for daily emotional support, reflection, and growth — not a clinical tool. If you're experiencing severe mental health challenges, we always encourage working with a qualified professional. Mia can be a beautiful complement to therapy, though.

How does Mia, the AI coach, actually work?

Mia is powered by a large language model carefully tuned to provide warm, emotionally intelligent support. She draws on your check-ins, journal entries, and conversation history to offer personalized guidance. She's designed to feel human — not robotic — while being clear that she's an AI.

Is my journal private and secure?

Absolutely. Your journal is encrypted and private. Premium users get access to the Secure Journal Vault with end-to-end encryption. We will never sell or share your personal data. Your inner world belongs only to you.

Can I try Premium before paying?

Yes. Every new account gets a free 7-day Premium trial — no credit card required. You'll have access to everything: unlimited Mia, all programs, full journaling insights, and the complete breathing library. You only pay if you love it.

What devices does MindShift+ work on?

MindShift+ is a fully responsive web app that works beautifully on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers. Native iOS and Android apps are coming soon.

How do the wellness programs work?

Each program has a daily structure: a short lesson (5 min read), a guided exercise (breathing, affirmation, or reflection), and a journaling prompt. Programs adapt to your pace — miss a day? No guilt. Pick up exactly where you left off.
