FAQ

Common questions,
answered clearly

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we are currently accepting new patients of all ages, from toddlers to elders. We offer both in-person appointments at our Milford and Boston locations, as well as secure telehealth sessions. Call us at (508) 619-1044 or book online to get started.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Cigna, Horizon BCBS, Independence Blue Cross, Meritain Health, Quest Behavioral Health, UnitedHealthcare, 1199SEIU, Carelon Behavioral Health, Coventry, and GEHA. We also offer self-pay options with transparent pricing. Contact us to verify your coverage.

What happens at the first appointment?

Your initial visit is a comprehensive psychiatric evaluation — typically 60–90 minutes. We'll review your mental health history, current symptoms, medications, and personal goals. From there, we'll collaborate on a personalized care plan that may include medication management, therapy, or both.

Do you offer telehealth appointments?

Yes. We offer secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth sessions via video for patients who prefer remote care. Flexible weekday evening slots are available to fit your schedule. Both new and existing patients can be seen via telehealth.

What is MindShift+ and do I need it?

MindShift+ is a free AI-powered wellness platform we offer to support patients between appointments. It includes an AI coach (Mia), guided journaling, breathing exercises, and mood tracking. It is not a replacement for clinical care — it's a tool to help you stay supported and engaged in your wellness journey between visits.

What ages do you treat?

We provide psychiatric care for all age groups — toddlers, children (6–10), preteens, teens, adults, and elders (65+). We also work with individuals, couples, and families.
